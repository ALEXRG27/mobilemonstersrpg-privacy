MobileMonstersRPG – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Alex Romagosa Girones ("we") built the game MobileMonstersRPG ("the App") for Android devices. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information the App handles

The current version is designed for offline, single-player play. It does not require an account or connect to a game server. The App does not collect or send personal information to the publisher.

The App saves gameplay and preference data on your device, such as game progress, inventory and equipment, companion team and progress, character appearance, and whether help content has been viewed. This information is used to save and restore your game locally. The publisher does not receive it through the App.

The inspected build includes no advertising, in-app purchase, analytics, crash-reporting, or other third-party data-collection SDK. It requests no Internet permission and no sensitive Android runtime permissions. This policy must be checked against the final release build and updated if a future version adds a network feature, permission, or SDK that handles data.

Storage, deletion, and backups

Gameplay data is stored in the App's local application data. You can remove that data by clearing MobileMonstersRPG's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the App. The App has no player account and the publisher has no account data to delete.

Android, a device manufacturer, or a backup service may back up application data if backup is enabled in the device or account settings. Such backups are handled under the relevant operating-system or service settings and are not accessible to the publisher through MobileMonstersRPG.

Sharing and security

The App does not transmit the locally stored gameplay data to the publisher or share it with third parties. Since the App does not transmit that data, encryption in transit does not apply. The publisher cannot guarantee the security of the device or of backup services configured by the user.

Children

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other players. The publisher does not receive personal information through the current offline release. The publisher intends the App for a general audience and not specifically for children. The target audience and any child-directed features must still be accurately declared in Google Play.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected in the policy before the changed practices are released.
